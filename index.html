Time Zone Guide & FAQ

IST vs CET/CEST Overview

India Standard Time (IST) is set to UTC+5:30 year-round without daylight saving adjustments. It applies across all states in India, serving major hubs like New Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore.

Netherlands Time follows Central European Time (CET, UTC+1) during winter and switches automatically to Central European Summer Time (CEST, UTC+2) during summer months across cities like Tilburg, Amsterdam, and Rotterdam.
